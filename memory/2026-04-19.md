We have no real-time equity estimation or Sharpe tracking. Running for 30 days blind. We'll know performance only after the fact.

### 4. P=15/M=1.50 is a marginal change
P=20/M=2.15 had 83% global pass. P=15/M=1.50 has 79.6% global pass. The production universe (Base5/NoDOGE) passes at 6/6 for both — so functionally equivalent for deployment. Turtle+Chandelier generates signals. In choppy 2026-type markets, we know it underperforms. But we never measured: what is the average trade duration in chop vs trend? How many trades per year in 2021 vs 2024? What's the cost of churn in flat markets?
