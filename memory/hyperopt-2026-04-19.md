## Finding 2: TURTLE_ATR_PERIOD Extended Sweep (5-100 step 1) — ZERO TRADES

Ran the full 96-value sweep (5-100 step 1) with production params.
**Result: 0 trades for all 96 values across all 9 universes.**

Root cause: Turtle entry signals (close > 21-bar max close) are extremely rare in BTC FDUSD data. The dataset contains BTC's local peak at bar ~222 (April 2023, ~$73K) and prices never exceeded this until very late in the dataset (bar ~794, late 2024, ~$125K). This means the 21-bar max close from early 2023 acted as a ceiling for Turtle entries for ~18 months.

**Impact:** Cannot use BTC FDUSD alone to validate Turtle parameters. Multi-symbol portfolio (ETH, SOL, XRP etc.) generates sufficient Turtle signals (924 trades, 79.6% pass).
