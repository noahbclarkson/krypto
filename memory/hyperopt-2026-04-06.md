## Parameter Optimized

**A/D momentum lookback period** (inline momentum = ad_line[current] - ad_line[current-period])

**Candidate values tested:** 10, 15, 20, 30, 42 bars  
**Universe:** Base5 (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA)  
**Method:** Walk-forward (7 windows, 252-bar train / 252-bar test, next-open entry, 21-bar hold, 0.1% taker)  
**Signal:** Long top-1 by A/D momentum; 0 trades when no positive momentum

## Robustness Check

1. **Pass/fail pattern is IDENTICAL across all periods except 10-bar.**  
   Same windows pass and fail regardless of period. This means the market regime determination is robust to the A/D lookback — the pass/fail is driven by market structure, not period tuning.

2. **Sharpe improvement (2.43 → 3.06) is driven by LARGER returns in W05 and W06, not by lower volatility.**  
   Longer lookbacks capture more sustained trend energy in W05/W06. W01 (mega-bull) is identical regardless of period because 20-bar and 30-bar A/D are both overwhelmed by the massive SOL/BTC move.

3. **Periods 30 and 42 are TIED on all metrics.**  
   This suggests we've hit the "smooth enough" regime where further extension adds no value. Period 30 is the minimal-change winner.

4. **Period 10 fails more windows (3/7 vs 4/7).**  
   Shorter lookbacks are noisier and fail in W05 where the trend is more gradual.

---

## Decision: UPDATE TO NEW DEFAULT

**New stable default: A/D period = 30**

- Sharpe improvement: +2.43 → +3.06 (+26% risk-adjusted improvement)
- Pass rate preserved: 4/7 (identical to baseline)
- Worst DD: unchanged (-69.1%)  
- Return improvement: +1003% → +1045% (+4%)

**Rationale:**
- Longer momentum windows smooth the A/D signal, capturing more durable trend energy
- Period 30 is the minimal-change winner — closer to original 20 than 42
- Same pass/fail window structure means no regime-detection regression
- 30-bar is also closer to standard academic lookbacks (often 20-40 bars for A/D variants)
