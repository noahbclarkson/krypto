## Conclusion

**CHAND_PERIOD is not sensitive.** All values from 15 to 50 pass at 100% on Base5. The 9-way check confirms the parameter is robust across all universes.

**Production recommendation:** Keep CP=28 (already frozen, stable). CP=17 is a viable alternative. The difference between CP=17 and CP=28 is <1 Sharpe point on Base5 and <1 pass point globally — this is not actionable.
