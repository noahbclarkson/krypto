## Biggest Blind Spot

**The live bot achieves 2.54x while the research harness achieves 176.79x on the same strategy family. The gap is not fees or execution — it's semantic.**

Breakdown of the 69x equity gap:
1. **Entry condition:** research harness uses strict prior-window Turtle (close > max close over prior window bars). Live bot uses current-inclusive window with equality-permissive entry. Different trade set.
2. **Volume ranking:** `VOL_LOOKBACK=92` in config but NOT used by `src/live/bot.rs`. The live bot does not rank by dollar volume — it processes symbols per event without relative ranking.
3. **Accounting:** live `BotState::record_trade` ignores position size. Research harnesses use size-aware economic accounting.
4. **Hedge overlay:** live bot applies USDT hedge overlay that research turtle_only_equity harness does not model.

**The 2.54x is the honest answer to "what would the coded bot have done."** The 176.79x is a research harness with different signal semantics. They are not comparable. We have been quoting the research number as production truth.

**The blind spot is compounded by the fact that we kept optimizing Turtle params (EP, CHAND, ATR, HOLD_MAX, VL, AP, LB, hedge threshold/size, weekend filter) on the research harness — not on the actual bot.** Every one of those optimizations improved the research harness while potentially having zero effect on the live bot (since VL, for example, is unused by the bot).
