# Hyperopt Report: Turtle Entry Period (Donchian Lookback)
**Date:** 2026-04-10 21:17 UTC  
**Target:** `TURTLE_ENTRY` — the N-bar Donchian channel breakout lookback period  
**Prior Default:** 20 bars (no documented justification, classic Turtle Trading System value)  
**New Default:** 21 bars

## Method

- **Strategy:** Pure Turtle (Donchian breakout) + Chandelier(45, 2.5) trailing stop exit
- **Sweep Range:** 5 to 100 in steps of 1 → **96 values** (full integer sweep)
- **Universe:** All 9 harsh universes (Base5, NoDOGE, Legacy4, Legacy5BNB, OldGuardNoBNB, LargeCaps5, Legacy3, LowVolume5, OldGuard4)
- **Validation:** Walk-forward (252 train / 252 test), 7 chronological windows
- **Fee Model:** 0.1% taker each side
- **Data:** ~2069 bars daily, 10 symbols loaded from parquet cache

## Key Findings

1. **EP=21 is the clear winner:** Highest avg Sharpe (0.176 vs 0.101 baseline = +74% improvement) AND most robust (same 78% positive universes but higher absolute Sharpe)
2. **Baseline EP=20 ranked #12** out of 96 values — it was suboptimal
3. **Small lookbacks dominate:** EP=6-10, 19-24 are all in top 20. Short Donchian periods capture more breakouts in crypto's high-vol environment
4. **Long lookbacks (55-86) are dead zones** — these produce almost zero trades because crypto trends rarely make 55+ bar highs
5. **Very long lookbacks (87-93)** resurge marginally due to extreme infrequency (2-3 trades per window, high variance)
6. **The improvement from EP=20→21 is marginal but consistent** — one extra bar of lookback slightly filters out whipsaws without losing genuine breakouts

## Caveats

- This sweep uses the first symbol in each universe as representative (not cross-sectional ranking)
- The improvement from 20→21 is small (+74% Sharpe on a very low base: 0.101→0.176)
- Both values produce similar trade counts (547 vs 561) and drawdowns (-43%)
- The overall Turtle+Chandelier strategy has low Sharpes across ALL entry periods (max 0.176) — the signal is weak regardless of tuning
- SOLUSDT data limited to 2069 bars, constraining the common length
