## Three Most Promising Unbuilt Ideas

### 1. 🎯 Multi-Timeframe Confirmation (4h → 1d Entry Filter)
**Idea:** Require 4h close above/below a 4h SMA(21) as a confirmation before taking a daily Turtle entry. NOT a regime switch — just a noise filter on entry.

**Why promising:** The 1h MR failed (0/6 on full history), but that was a standalone MR strategy. Using 4h trend direction as an entry filter for daily Turtle is different. The 4h timeframe has better signal-to-noise than 1h. A 4h filter could reduce whipsaw entries in choppy markets without destroying valid breakouts.

**Risk:** We tried ATR entry filter (mult>0 destroys pass rate). 4h SMA filter is different — it's confirming the daily breakout is aligned with a shorter-term trend, not requiring a vol gate. Needs a dedicated walk-forward harness.

**Honest assessment:** Medium probability. The mechanism is theoretically sound (4h trend alignment before daily entry). The risk is that it becomes another trade-starving filter like the ATR entry multiplier. Only way to know is to build the harness.

---

### 2. 🎯 Equity Portfolio Integration (SPY/QQQ + Crypto Multi-Asset)
**Idea:** Replace pure crypto universe with a curated multi-asset universe: BTC, ETH, SOL, SPY, QQQ, GLD. All validated Turtle+Chandelier candidates. SPY/QQQ/GLD passed the OOS walk-forward. Combine them in a single Turtle portfolio.

**Why promising:** Crypto-only Turtle has extreme drawdowns (62.6% on full history). Adding SPY/QQQ (lower vol, same Turtle edge) reduces portfolio drawdown. SPY and QQQ are negatively correlated with crypto in stress events (2022 crash: SPY -19%, BTC -64%). The combination could reduce MaxDD by 10-15pp without proportional return reduction.

**Risk:** SPY/QQQ/GLD are US equity products — not 24/7 like crypto. Overnight gaps could create signal artifacts. Weekend/holiday closes mean we enter on Monday open after a Friday signal. This needs careful examination of daily bar data alignment.

**Honest assessment:** High probability of improving Sharpe/MaxDD ratio. The edge on equities is proven (88% pass on SPY). Multi-asset walk-forward harness is the only missing piece.

---

### 3. 🎯 Drawdown-Adaptive Exposure (New Mechanism, Not Position Sizing)
**Concept:** Reduce NOT position size but **signal frequency** — when portfolio drawdown > 15%, require a higher-entry threshold (e.g., EP=21 → EP=25) or add a 4h SMA confirmation filter. When drawdown reverts, revert params. This is different from "reduce position size" (which failed) — it's a signal-quality tightening, not a risk-parity adjustment.

**Why promising:** Every position-sizing overlay (USDT hedge, BTC scalar, drawdown trigger) failed by changing the risk budget. This changes signal quality — it makes the system require cleaner breakouts when already underwater. The hypothesis: whipsaw losses in chop come from entering on marginal breakouts that fail. Tightening the entry threshold in drawdown reduces the marginal entries that get stopped out.

**Why cautious:** ATR entry filter (similar concept) failed — mult>0 destroyed pass rate. But ATR filter was a vol gate, not a drawdown gate. The drawdown-adaptive mechanism is qualitatively different.

**Honest assessment:** Medium-low probability. The theoretical mechanism is plausible but ATR filter result is concerning. Worth one dedicated walk-forward test, then graveyard if it fails.
