## Biggest Blind Spots

### Blind Spot 1: We Only Tested in a 2017-2026 Bull Market

The entire Turtle+Chandelier validation dataset (9 universes, 54 windows) spans 2017-2026. This is predominantly a bull market era for crypto. The pre-2021 stress test (21/21 pass) is the exception, not the rule. We have almost no data on how Turtle performs in a sustained multi-year bear market (like 2018 or a hypothetical 2027-2029 bear).

The 2022 data point (+10.9% Turtle, Sharpe 0.51) is informative but brief — the bear market was punctuated by sharp recoveries that Chandelier caught. A slow-motion 80% drawdown over 3 years (no recovery) would look very different.

### Blind Spot 2: We're Over-Confident in Our Sharpe Numbers

- Walk-forward Sharpe 6.29 ≠ daily equity Sharpe 1.26
- Fee-adjusted 5.25 is still inflated by mega-bull windows
- 80-trade reliability threshold: we're at N=80 for Base5 — barely above the minimum
- The "best result" framing is dangerous: if we cherry-pick the best windows, best universes, best params, we look incredible. The AVERAGE window is less impressive.

### Blind Spot 3: Maker vs Taker Is the Biggest Unmeasured Variable

We confirmed ~63-70% maker fill rate in backtests. But:
- In a live崩盘 (crash), maker fill rate drops precipitously (W05 FTX: 70%→56%)
- In high-vol regimes, spreads widen and maker depth thins
- The fee gap (taker vs maker) is the difference between Sharpe 3.86 and 4.68 — a 21% performance swing
- This is the #1 risk to live performance and we have ZERO live data on it

### Blind Spot 4: We Haven't Tested Exchange-Specific Fragility

All data is from Binance. We don't know:
- If the strategy works on Kraken, OKX, Bybit with different liquidity profiles
- If maker rebates vary enough to change the relative attractiveness of entry vs exit timing
- If FTX-specific effects (BTC in October 2021) create a structural advantage/disadvantage we haven't modeled
