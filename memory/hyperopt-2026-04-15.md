## Why p=2?

A/D momentum = A/D line(t) - A/D line(t-N). A shorter period (p=2) captures:
- Faster accumulation/distribution pressure changes
- More responsive signals in crypto's high-volatility environment
- Earlier entry into emerging trends

The standalone A/D sweep (p=1..100, fixed 21-bar hold) found p=70/74 as best. But turtle_ad uses Turtle+A/D+SMA200 ensemble — the A/D momentum period interacts with Turtle's entry signals and the BTC SMA200 regime filter. For this combined strategy, p=2 is optimal.
