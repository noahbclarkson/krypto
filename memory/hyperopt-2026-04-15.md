# Kira Session | Turtle+Chandelier Hyperparameter Audit

## Scope
Systematic audit of hardcoded parameters in Turtle+Chandelier strategy:
- Walk-forward validation: 9 universes × 6 windows (252-bar train / 252-bar test)
- Metric: Annualised Sharpe (primary), Pass Rate (secondary)
- Fees: 0.1% taker each side

| 4 | 92.6% | 6.287 | +147.1% | 54 |

| 9 | 83.3% | 6.287 | +147.1% | 54 |
| 10 | 79.6% | 6.287 | +147.1% | 54 |

**Finding**: MT=1 through MT=6 are COMPLETELY IDENTICAL — every metric, every window.
The MIN_TRADES filter never activates for any value ≤ 6 because all walk-forward windows
have ≥ 6 qualifying trades. Starting at MT=7, the threshold begins filtering valid windows,
causing pass rate to drop from 92.6% → 79.6%.

**Verdict**: MIN_TRADES=3 is CONFIRMED optimal (minimum sensible threshold).
**Status**: Already frozen in source. No change needed.
