### Interpretation

- **HM=1** wins on Sharpe (2.134) but at catastrophic trade frequency (7,378 trades vs 2,525 for HM=15). Sharpe inflation from very large N — 7378 trades normalizes the mean/std of returns better than 2525.
- **HM=8–15 plateau:** Sharpe stable around 1.54–1.61, trades around 2,500–3,000. These are the practical operating range.
- **HM=15** (current production default): good Sharpe, reasonable trade count, well within plateau. Trade quality (win rate 45.3%, return +38.2%) is solid.
- **HM=30–100:** identical results (2,373 trades, 45.0% WR). HOLD_MAX never binds above ~25 bars because Turtle ATR fires first.

### Mechanism

Turtle ATR(24,2.0) trailing stop fires at approximately bar 8–15 in trending regimes. HOLD_MAX only binds in non-trending sideways chop where ATR doesn't trigger. Since all HOLD_MAX values pass 100%, the parameter is a safety backstop, not a primary driver.
