## Honest Overall Assessment

**The single most important thing:** Live testnet data. Without it, we're flying blind. Every additional hyperopt run on historical data increases over-fit risk without increasing confidence.

**What we should stop doing:** Stop optimizing historical parameters. Stop documenting. Stop auditing. Ship the live bot and get real data.
