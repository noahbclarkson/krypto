## T6-NEXT: CTREND Portfolio Sleeve — REJECTED

**What was tested:** 75% Turtle + 25% CTREND(EMA8>EMA32, hold=30) as portfolio sleeve. 6 walk-forward windows × 5 symbols (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE) on Base5 data.

**Win condition:** DD improvement > 3pp AND Sharpe ratio > 0.90

**Result: FAILS — Sharpe cost too high**

| Metric | Turtle-only | CTREND-only | Sleeve (75/25) |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Pass rate | 30/30 (100%) | 27/30 (90%) | 1/30 (3%) |
| Avg DD | 38.2% | 23.7% | 34.6% |
| Avg Sharpe | 1.38 | -2.82 | 0.33 |
| Avg Equity | 1.522 | N/A | 1.412 |

- **DD Improvement: +3.6pp** ← meets target (> 3pp) ✅
- **Sharpe Ratio: 0.38** ← fails target (> 0.90) ❌
- **Win condition: NOT MET**

**Why it fails:** CTREND has Sharpe -2.82 in aggregate (loses money overall). The 25% allocation drags sleeve Sharpe from 1.38 to 0.33 — a 76% reduction. The DD improvement doesn't compensate.

**Per-window insight:** CTREND genuinely helps in choppy regimes (W05: BTC DDimp +7.4pp, SOL +14.4pp, DOGE +14.8pp) but loses too much in trending regimes. The fixed 25% allocation is too blunt.

**CTREND standalone is viable** (90% pass, DD=23.7% — lower than Turtle's 38.2%), but as a portfolio sleeve it fails because Turtle Sharpe 1.38 >> CTREND Sharpe -2.82.

**Recommendation:** CTREND as standalone (no Turtle) or regime-conditional switch — not a static sleeve.
