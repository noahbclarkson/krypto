## Brutal Critique

### 1. We Are Overfitting to Bull Markets

Our 83% global pass rate (45/54) is built on a specific bull-era market structure. Pre-2021 stress = **67.9%** (19/28). 2026 YTD live = **-22.7%** vs BTC +12.7%. The strategy works when markets trend. It fails when they don't.

**The 2021-2022 chop problem is structural.** The Chandelier trailing stop fires too aggressively in sideways markets, converting winning trades into many small losses. We've tried 10+ approaches to fix this:
- Vol-adaptive Chandelier: tied, no benefit (GRAVEYARD)
- 4h timeframe: structurally broken (5% pass)
- Position scaling: degrades returns (GRAVEYARD)
- Correlation filter: no help (GRAVEYARD)
- BTC trend scalar: never activates (GRAVEYARD)
- Turtle-only exit: doesn't fix chop (S6 confirmed)
- CTREND portfolio: Sharpe destroyed by CTREND component

**Verdict:** The chop problem may not be fixable with the Turtle+Chandelier framework. We need a genuinely different strategy class for choppy regimes, not another overlay on the same signal.

## Top 3 Unbuilt Ideas (From strategy-ideas.md)

### 1. S8: Donchian Entry vs Turtle Entry — GENUINELY UNTESTED
Turtle uses `close > max(close, high)` (21-bar max of either). Donchian uses `close > highest(high)` (strictest definition — highest high ever). This is the original Richard Dennis 1983 Turtle system entry signal. Never tested in our framework. Might produce fewer but higher-quality signals.

**Why it could matter:** The entry signal space is almost completely unexplored. We've spent all our time on exit optimization. Entry is the other half of the problem.

### 2. S9: W05 Live Failure Diagnostic — CRITICAL, STILL UNDONE

**Why it matters:** -22.7% YTD vs BTC +12.7% is the #1 credibility problem. We can't claim the strategy works live if it's losing in a nominally bull market.

### 3. Regime-Conditional CTREND Switching — NOT THE SAME AS S7
S7 tested Turtle(75%)+CTREND(25%) fixed portfolio. That destroyed Sharpe. But **regime-conditional switching** (if ATR percentile < 25th → enter CTREND instead of Turtle) is different. CTREND has 73% OOS pass and works in choppy regimes where Turtle whipsaws. This is the complement, not the blend.

**Why it matters:** If CTREND works in chop (where Turtle fails), conditional switching could solve the #1 structural problem.
