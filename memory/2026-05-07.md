### 2. The Equity is Fragile — Top-10 Concentration Problem

From HOF: **Top-10 = 90.9% of compounded log return. Equity without top-10 = 1.10x.**

This means the entire edge lives in roughly 10 trades out of 286. The remaining 276 trades compound to only +10%. 

**Why this matters:** The strategy is structurally a lottery ticket. One of those top-10 winners becoming a loser (or being blocked by a new filter) silently destroys 90% of historical return. We've verified this experimentally:
- VL=92 top-3 gate: kills 8/10 top winners
- ATR_RANK>=24: kills 5/10 top winners
- ATR_RANK>=65: kills 8/10 top winners
- T72 volume gate: collapsed equity to 1.01x

The hedge size retune (HSM=0.25) was framed as defensive robustness, but it doesn't address this structural fragility. We're essentially hoping the next 10 big winners survive whatever we do next.

**Honest assessment:** 2.77x over 1,796 days is real, but it's a very specific convex outcome from a very specific set of market conditions. The Sharpe 1.03 looks reasonable but masks this concentration risk.

### 6. Walk-Forward Sharpe Is Still Being Misused

**The problem:** Anyone new reading our docs sees "Sharpe 5.46" and thinks the strategy is exceptional. The honest number is 1.03.

2021 and 2022 are the regimes that look LEAST like the mega-bull 2017/2019/2020/2023/2024 that dominate our total return. The strategy passes walk-forward on Base5 (which includes some chop years) but the per-year numbers show real degradation.

**The concern:** Our validation is biased toward bull-market outperformance because the equity curve started in 2018 (crypto's worst bear) and 2021 was a mega-bull before the crash. The equity weighting of early years (where Turtle dominated dramatically) inflates the overall Sharpe.

**We have no genuine bear-market stress test at the portfolio level.** Single-symbol W04 (2021-2022 chop) passes, but portfolio-level sustained drawdown over 12+ months hasn't been explicitly tested.
