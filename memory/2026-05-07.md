## Top 3 Most Promising Unbuilt Ideas

### 1. C17: Consecutive-Bar Momentum Filter (highest practical priority)

**Why promising:** Requires 2 consecutive closes above Turtle entry level — not a parameter sweep but a qualitatively new entry confirmation mechanism. Prior ATR-based filters (ATR_ENTRY_MULT) were rejected because they filtered too aggressively. Consecutive-bar is gentler — it only requires the breakout to sustain one extra bar.

**Risk:** May reduce trade count enough to hurt Sharpe. Must be tested.

**Test:** Turtle baseline vs Turtle+consecutive-bar. 9 universes × 6 windows. Must beat baseline on pass rate AND Sharpe. If either degrades, close permanently.

### 2. C16: Regime-Conditional Chandelier Multiplier (genuinely novel mechanism)

**Why promising:** Binary regime (SMA21 vs SMA200) switches 2-4x per year — fast enough to be useful, not so fast it's noise. The failed vol-contingent attempt used 21-bar realized vol rank, which barely crossed thresholds. Regime classification is fundamentally different.

**Risk:** Regime classifier is itself unstable in chop (flips frequently). Need to check actual flip frequency before testing.

**Test:** 3 configs (static 2.0, static 2.30, regime-conditional) × 9 universes × 6 windows. Both static baselines must be beaten on pass rate and Sharpe.

### 3. C18: Maker-Fill Rate Stress Test (critical risk quantification)

**Why promising:** We have zero quantification of equity at maker-fill rates below 70%. FTX crash gave us one data point. A sustained bear market (2022) could degrade maker fill to 40% or lower. We cannot claim the strategy is "deployable" without knowing the fee-adjusted equity range.

**Risk:** This is a post-hoc calculation on existing equity CSV — no new data download needed. It should take <1 hour to build.

**Test:** Apply fee adjustment at maker_fill ∈ {0.30, 0.35, 0.40, ..., 0.80} to exact-live equity. Output: table of equity_mult and fee_adj_sharpe at each step.
