# A/D Momentum Entry Threshold Hyperopt (0.0 to 50.0x median)
*Date: 2026-04-09*

## Summary

Audited the A/D Momentum strategy. The original implementation used a naive hardcoded entry threshold of `momentum > 0.0`, meaning any tiny positive shift triggered an entry. I hypothesized that filtering out weak momentum changes would improve signal quality and reduce drawdown.

I swept entry thresholds from 0.0 to 50.0x the median absolute A/D momentum across 3000 bars and all 9 universes (walk-forward 252/252, fixed Chandelier ATR exit).

## Results

The sweep proves that filtering by a specific threshold improves out-of-sample performance:

*   **Winner:** 15.0x Median A/D Momentum (Sharpe = 0.859, DD = 34.6%, Ret = +78.3%)
*   **Runner-up:** 10.0x Median (Sharpe = 0.835, DD = 36.8%)
*   **Baseline (0.0x):** Sharpe = 0.726, DD = 41.3%, Ret = +67.8%

Requiring momentum to be at least 15x the median absolute bar-to-bar change drastically reduces whipsaws, increasing Sharpe while preserving upside and cutting drawdown by ~6%.
