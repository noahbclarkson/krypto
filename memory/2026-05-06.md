### Our Biggest Blind Spots

**1. We don't actually know why research = 176.79x and live = 2.54x**
T65 measured the gap. T69 tried a semantic patch and made it WORSE. We have not explained the mechanism. Is it:
- (a) The dual Chandelier exit vs Turtle-only exit producing very different trade durations and compounding?
- (b) Position sizing difference (equal size vs dollar-volume weighted)?
- (c) The account equity model (economic mark-to-market vs realized-only)?
- (d) All three simultaneously?
Without explaining this, every parameter we validated on the research harness (EP=21, AP=17, LB=41, VL=92, etc.) could be partially or wholly irrelevant to the live bot path.

**2. Top-trade concentration is dangerously high and underappreciated**
- Top 5 trades = 57.8% of log return
- Top 10 trades = 91.5% of log return
- 288 of 298 trades collectively produce only 8.5% of total return
- A filter that accidentally removes 2-3 of the top-10 winners would reduce 2.55x to ~1.5x. We have no systematic audit of what conditions produce those winners, so we can't protect them.

**3. We are not testing in bear/deaccumulation regimes**
2026 YTD: strategy is -22.7% while BTC is +12.7%. The current regime (bear whipsaw) is the hardest for Turtle. We have documented that Sharpe is inversely correlated with bull-market strength, but we keep measuring success in bull windows (W03 mega-bull dominates the equity curve). The "2.55x in 1795 days" headline is mostly the 2018-2021 bull compounding; the 2022-2026 period is much weaker.

**4. The USDT hedge is documented as inert but still in the code**
HEDGE_ATR_PCT tested 101 values → all identical. HEDGE_SIZE_MULT swept 13 values → pure risk dial (no Sharpe improvement). The hedge overlay is dead code in the live execution path, but it's still discussed in docs and strategy-ideas.md as if it contributes. This is confusing for future-us.

**5. ATR_RANK=5: configured but not fully verified in live path**
- `src/live/bot.rs` has `atr_rank_threshold: 5.0` in config
- T65 harness correctly uses `check_atr_rank_entry_filter` with AP=17/LB=42/T=5
- But the HOF.md notes "VOL_LOOKBACK configured but unused by bot.rs" — this is a clear sign the research and live paths have diverged
