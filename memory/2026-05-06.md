## Are Our Metrics Real or Are We Curve-Fitting?

**Honest assessment:**

1. **Daily equity Sharpe 1.01 is probably real.** It comes from exact-live replay with exact live bot code, no look-ahead, realistic fees. 2.81x over 1795 days across 6 symbols is modest but honest.

2. **Walk-forward Sharpe 5-6 is probably inflated as a *per-window average* methodology.** PLAN.md correctly documents that walk-forward Sharpe = mean of per-window Sharpe ratios (mean/std×√252 then averaged). This methodology inflates the number. We know this and label it correctly, so it's not fraud — but it's also not comparable to the daily equity Sharpe.

3. **The 176.79x research harness is a diagnostic system, not a performance claim.** It has 99.5% MaxDD and 156 trades vs live bot's 298 trades — a completely different risk profile. We document it as diagnostic only.

Every Turtle-family parameter (EP, CHAND_P, CHAND_M, HM, ATR, ATR_MULT, CAP, VL, FRESHNESS, HEDGE_ATR_P, HEDGE_SIZE) has been validated on this grid. We've effectively "learned" the grid's behavior through repetition. **We have no out-of-sample universe that wasn't part of the optimization history.**
