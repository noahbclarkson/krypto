## Biggest Blind Spots

### Blind Spot 1: We Only Know Our Strategy Works In The Configured Universes
Base5 (BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/DOGE/ADA) passes 6/6 windows. But the last 6 commits were all tested on the same 9-universe grid we've used since April. We're optimizing parameters on a fixed validation grid that we've effectively "learned" through repeated use. The grid itself may be over-fit to our specific parameter choices.

**We have no out-of-sample universe that wasn't part of the optimization history.**

### Blind Spot 2: We Don't Know If Maker-Fill Assumption Holds In A Down Market
Our maker-fill assumption (70.6%) comes from a microstructure analyzer run on historical Binance data. But what happens to maker-fill rate during a crash? The W05 FTX window showed 70% maker-fill even during the crash — that's encouraging. But we only have one crash data point. We don't know if maker-fill degrades to 40% or 20% in a sustained bear market, which would raise our fee drag from ~22% to something higher.

### Blind Spot 3: We Are Measuring Sharpe On Daily Returns But Describing It Like A Total Return
Daily account Sharpe 0.95 over 1795 days means the strategy beats buy-hold on a risk-adjusted basis. But 2.55x over ~5 years is only 20% annualized — not extraordinary for crypto. We are comparing this to the research harness (176.79x) which used a different system (Turtle-only, different accounting) and calling the gap a "problem." But 2.55x is actually a reasonable result for a risk-managed trend-following strategy.

The research harness number inflated our expectations. The live bot number (2.55x) is honest.

### Blind Spot 4: We Keep Starting Sessions With "Read the docs" Instead Of "Run the code"
The session startup instruction says: "Read PLAN.md, MEMORY.md, strategy-ideas.md, then check git." Every session begins as a reading comprehension exercise. We're getting very good at understanding our own documentation. We're getting no better at understanding our code.
