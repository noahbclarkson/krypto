## Key Finding: MECHANICAL EFFECT, NOT SIGNAL

The FC>50 pass rate improvement is from **blocking re-entries**, NOT from any signal improvement:
1. FC=53 blocks ~67% of entries from the base (3118→1020 trades)
2. FC=53 with exact-live: ~286→~94 trades (67% reduction)
3. Fewer trades = lower variance = higher pass rate mechanically
4. **This is trade frequency management, not alpha**

## Decision: NOT PROMOTING

- **FRESHNESS_COOLDOWN=0 remains production default**
- FC>50 effect is mechanical (blocks re-entries), not signal-based
- The exact-live baseline (286 trades) is already well-calibrated
- Adding cooldown would cut trade count by ~67%, sacrificing convexity
- With HOLD_MAX=15, the existing strategy properly manages duration
