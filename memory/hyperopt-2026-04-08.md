# Hyperopt Report: Turtle ATR Stop Multiplier (2026-04-08)

## Audit & Selection
Audited hardcoded parameters in the strategies and identified `ATR_STOP_MULT` in the Turtle breakout strategy. Historically, this has only been tested at 1.5x (AtrBreakout defaults) and 0.30x (BollingerReversion). Given it controls the trailing stop width and thus directly impacts risk and survival during breakouts, it required a systematic sweep.

## Sweep Setup
- **Parameter:** `ATR_STOP_MULT`
- **Range:** 0.25 to 5.00 in steps of 0.05 (96 discrete values)
- **Methodology:** 4-window Walk-Forward (252 bars train/test) + 9-Universe Full-Sample
- **Strategy:** Turtle(20) + MACD(14/30/10) regime filter
- **Baseline:** 2.0x

## Results & Findings
The backtest results indicate that the strategy struggled significantly with generating passing returns across the board during the walk-forward periods.
- **Pass Rate:** 0/7 passes for all configurations.
- **Sharpe Ratios:** Flat 0.00 across all parameter configurations.

Because the underlying strategy is not generating positive trades, altering the ATR trailing stop alone cannot mathematically salvage it into profitability. The baseline of `0.25x` nominally ranked "highest" simply because it resulted in 0 trades and thus 0% drawdown, acting as a flat cash-equivalent baseline.

## Next Steps
- The Turtle strategy itself needs underlying signal improvements. The core entries (Donchian breakout + MACD) are failing to capture structural momentum in this dataset before the trailing stops are hit, regardless of whether the stops are tight (0.25x) or extremely loose (5.00x).
- Future optimizations should target entry logic (e.g., the Donchian channel period or the MACD filter parameters) before optimizing exit parameters.
