## Biggest Blind Spot: Sharpe Metric Incompatibility

**Every strategy reports "Sharpe" but they measure different things:**
- Walk-forward per-window Sharpe: mean(per_window_sharpe_ratios) — inflates by ~3-7x vs equity Sharpe
- Daily equity Sharpe: compounded daily returns — honest risk-adjusted metric
- Milestone-aggregated Sharpe: mean of period returns — meaningless for strategy comparison

**This is the single most important reporting problem to fix.**

Fix: `daily_progress.csv` needs a `sharpe_methodology` column. Or all strategies should be recomputed on daily equity Sharpe (the only honest comparison).
