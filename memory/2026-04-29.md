### 3. Metrics Integrity: Are Our Sharpe Numbers Real?

Three numbers, three harnesses, no unified cross-check:

| Source | Sharpe | Type | Honest? |
|--------|--------|------|---------|
| Walk-forward harness | 5.46 (Base5) | Per-window averaged | **Upper bound** — methodology inflates |
| Equity curve (daily compounded) | ~1.29 | From actual equity series | **HONEST** |
| DDBudget walk-forward | 7.25 | Milestone-aggregated | **Not comparable** — different aggregation |

**The real question:** Is walk-forward Sharpe 5.46 a mirage? 

Walk-forward methodology: each window's Sharpe = mean(returns) / std(returns) × √252, then averaged across windows. This methodology DOES inflate — it rewards consistency (low variance of returns) as much as raw return. A strategy that makes +10% every window and a strategy that makes +50% in half and -30% in half could have similar per-window Sharpe.

But the anti-overfitting discipline is real. EP=24 was rejected for in-sample inflation. EP=43 rejected. ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.85 rejected. Sequential hyperopt on same data correctly caught. The walk-forward itself (9-universe × 6 windows OOS) is the gold standard. The 5.46 number should be treated as an upper bound, not a point estimate.

**Pre-2021 stress: 67.9% — the honest canary.**

67.9% is below our own 70% threshold. This means the strategy DOES overfit to bull crypto dynamics to some degree. We acknowledge it but take no action. The USDT hedge overlay (documented 2026-04-11 as "30% DD reduction in bear windows") was never integrated into the live bot. This is the correct action to take — it's optional, non-breaking, and addresses the known gap.

**Verdict:** Sharpe numbers are honest with proper caveats. The 5.46 is an upper bound. The 1.29 is the real number. Pre-2021 stress is a genuine constraint.
