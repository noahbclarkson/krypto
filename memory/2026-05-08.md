### T86 Vol-Scaled Kelly Position Sizing — EXACT-LIVE TEST

**Decision: CONCEPT CLOSED.**

Tested per-symbol inverse-vol Kelly sizing: `size = (1/POSITION_CAP) × (median_vol / sym_vol) × HEDGE_SIZE_MULT` clamped to [0.5, 2.0].

**Result vs baseline:**
| Metric | Vol-Scaled Kelly | Baseline | Delta |
|--------|-----------------|----------|-------|
| Final equity | **2.2822x** | 2.76x | -0.48x (-17%) |
| Daily Sharpe | **0.87** | 1.02 | -0.15 |
| Max DD | **33.3%** | 22.3% | +11pp |
| Annual return | **18.2%** | 22.9% | -4.7pp |
| Trades | 286 | 286 | — |
| Win rate | 46.9% | 46.9% | — |
| Top-10 concentration | 91.7% | 91% | — |

**Mechanism failed.** Vol scaling consistently undersized the highest-conviction winners (high-vol trending symbols = largest moves). vol_scale > 1.5 on 34 trades with avg positive return, but vol_scale < 0.5 on 28 trades with avg NEGATIVE return. The inverse-vol mechanism systematically reduced exposure to the symbols with the most volatile (and largest) moves — exactly backwards of the intended mechanism.

**T73 guardrail:** equity_without_top10 = 1.6446x vs baseline 1.09x — the mechanism DID preserve top winners (higher without-top10 equity because vol scaling reduced the noise trades). However, the MAIN effect was worse: vol scaling cut winners too aggressively and didn't cut losers enough.

**Key insight:** The top winners (XRP 2021-07-28 +37.8%, SOL 2023-01-11 +48.1%, DOGE 2022-10-28 +45.1%) were HIGH-vol assets during their winning periods. Inverse-vol scaling reduced their position sizes precisely when they needed maximum exposure.

**Anti-pattern confirmed:** Inverse-vol sizing is a textbook mechanism that sounds good in theory but destroys convexity in practice. The asymmetric nature of crypto momentum (big winners are also high-vol) means vol-scaling systematically anti-leverages the tail.

**Status:** CONCEPT CLOSED. GRAVEYARD.
