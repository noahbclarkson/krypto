### 4. Our Biggest Blind Spots

**Blind spot #1 — Maker-fill uncertainty is the biggest unmeasured risk.**
We assume 0.04% taker on all trades. Live FDUSD perpetuals have 0.00% maker. Prior analysis estimated 40-70% maker fill rate, but we have NO live feedback. If maker fill is persistently low (e.g., 20%), our real Sharpe is closer to 0.6, not 1.03. This is the single variable that could invalidate the entire strategy's deployability case.

**Blind spot #2 — 2026 YTD is a regime we have not seen in backtest.**
2026 year-to-date: Turtle -22.7%, BTC +12.7%, Sharpe -5.31. This is a whipsaw/chop regime where trend-following loses money. Our historical backtest covers 2018-2025, which includes 2022 bear (Turtle +10.9%, BTC -45.6%) and 2021 chop (Turtle +35.5%, BTC -16.5%). But 2026 is distinct: BTC is up while Turtle loses — a divergence pattern not explicitly tested. Chandelier(P=7, M=2.30) exits fast in chop, but if entries are being stopped out repeatedly, the strategy could produce long strings of small losses.

**Blind spot #3 — We test exclusively on trending/high-beta crypto.**
Base5 = BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA — all high-beta, all trend-following-native assets. UNI (hold-out) fails 5/6. MATIC (also hold-out) passes 6/6. The edge is in high-beta trending crypto, not general crypto. We have NOT tested on mean-reversion-dominated assets (e.g., stablecoin pairs, low-vol assets). Claiming cross-universe generalization is false.

**Blind spot #4 — M1 monitor runs in vacuum.**
We built an equity trajectory monitor and then... nothing. We don't see its output. We don't know if the bot is drifting, underperforming, or hitting alert thresholds. This is the operational infrastructure equivalent of building a smoke detector and never connecting it to the alarm.

**Blind spot #5 — Position sizing is frozen at HSM=0.25 with no adaptive mechanism.**
We tuned HSM from 0.55 to 0.25 based on robustness (86.7% pass), but it's still a fixed parameter. Vol-scaled sizing could improve risk-adjusted returns, but we haven't tested it. The fixed HSM applies the same haircut to all positions regardless of their realized vol.
