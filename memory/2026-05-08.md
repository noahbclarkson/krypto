## Are Our Metrics Real?

**Daily account Sharpe 1.02 / Equity 2.75x — YES, real.**
Source: `live_bot_exact_equity.rs` exact replay of `src/live/bot.rs`. No look-ahead. Honest.

**Annualised return 22.9% — MISLEADING.**
The equity spans 1,796 days. 2.75x = 175% total return. Annualised ≈ 22.9% — correct arithmetic, but the yearly decomposition reveals the distribution is extremely skewed:

| Year | Return | Verdict |
|------|--------|---------|
| 2021 | +66.6% | Mega-bull prop |
| 2022 | -20.1% | Lost money |
| 2023 | +39.0% | BTC-led |
| 2024 | +22.8% | Modest |
| 2025 | +25.1% | Modest |
| 2026 | -3.3% | Losing money YTD |

The strategy had a great 2021 (propped by mega-bull) and a terrible 2022. In 2024 and 2025 — the last two "normal" years without a mega-bull — returns were 22.8% and 25.1%. That's acceptable, not exceptional. The 22.9% headline hides the fact that 2022 was a 20% loss.

**Walk-forward Sharpe ~5.5 — INFLATED ~5x. NOT real.**
Per-window averaged Sharpe ratio inflates dramatically vs daily compounded account Sharpe. T80 OOS hold-out (UNI/MATIC/AVAX) showed avg Sharpe 0.149 — nowhere near 5.5. The walk-forward harness systematically selects for symbols and windows where the strategy works. The 100% Base5 pass rate is the floor of expectation, not evidence of generalization.

**Top-10 = 91.4% of log return.** Alarming and real. Equity without top-10 trades = 1.09x. The entire equity story depends on capturing 2-3 big trending moves per year. In years where those moves don't come, annual return is flat to negative.

**Fee-adjusted Sharpe range [0.6, 1.3] — we report 1.02 as point estimate.**
This is a known anti-pattern. We know maker fill could be 30-80%, which shifts daily Sharpe from 0.6 to 1.3. Reporting 1.02 as a point estimate is misleading when the range is 2x wide.

## Biggest Blind Spots

### 1. We only test in a "learned" universe
Base5 = BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/DOGE/ADA — pairs we chose specifically because they trend. T80 confirmed: UNI fails 5/6, AVAX borderline 4/6. MATIC passes 6/6 but data ends 2024-09-10 — doesn't cover 2025-2026 regime. The "100% pass rate on Base5" is the minimum standard, not evidence of generalization. We have no idea how the strategy performs on genuinely unseen pairs.

### 2. We have never shipped anything live
After ~3 months of v2-rewrite and hundreds of commits, nothing has touched real or testnet capital. The entire project is simulation. Every metric is an upper bound. We don't know if the exchange API integration, position accounting, or order execution path even works.

### 3. Fee model is the biggest unmeasured variable
Our daily Sharpe 1.02 assumes 4bp taker/side. The maker fill rate is unknown (30-80% range). At 30% maker: Sharpe ~0.6. At 80% maker: Sharpe ~1.3. We report a point estimate when the range is 2x wide. This is a known anti-pattern (PLAN.md says "report fee-adjusted as range, not point estimate") but we keep doing it.

### 4. Snapshot hygiene is a persistent problem
Every session produces new CSV/MD files. Old ones are not archived or deleted. The T84 run overwrote `live_bot_exact_equity.csv` and `.md` with LB=147 results. After the LB=147 revert, the snapshot was not regenerated. This creates a minefield of stale data. Someone reading `live_bot_exact_equity.md` today sees LB=147 / 2.10x — which is not the current production state (LB=252 → 2.75x).

### 5. We are optimizing the wrong thing
The project has been in "research closed, ops phase" since 2026-05-04. In the 4 days since, the productive output has been: T84 (confirmed gap, no new capability), T85 (chart of a confirmation), and data refreshes. We keep re-confirming known problems instead of building the infrastructure for actual deployment.
