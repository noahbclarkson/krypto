### Critique #3: The 91% Convex Tail Is Our Biggest Unresolved Problem

Top-10 trades = 91% of compounded log return. We know the 10 winners. We know their entry conditions. We CANNOT explain the mechanism that generates the tail.

Two largest winners: SOL 2023-01-11 and DOGE 2022-10-28. Both entered in LOW-VOL BTC Q1 regimes. This is exactly the kind of regime the ATR_RANK gate is supposed to filter out — but these two entries slipped through the T=5 gate anyway (they were valid because BTC vol was low enough). So the gate works "on average" but "accidentally" includes the exact entries that become the biggest winners.

**The dangerous interpretation:** "We found a regime where the best trades happen, and it's low-vol BTC regimes."
**The accurate interpretation:** "We have a non-stationary gate that sometimes includes the right trades and sometimes doesn't. The fact that it included SOL 2023-01-11 and DOGE 2022-10-28 was luck, not design."

Any new entry filter must prove it preserves these 2 trades. We can't do that without understanding WHY they worked. We don't.
