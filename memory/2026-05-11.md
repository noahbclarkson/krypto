## Biggest Blind Spots

### Blind Spot 1: We don't know if the top-10 winners are luck or skill.

SOL 2023-01-11 and DOGE 2022-10-28 are the two largest contributors. Both entered in low-vol regimes. We cannot explain mechanistically why Turtle entry in a low-vol BTC environment produces outsized winners in SOL and DOGE. The T73 audit documented the winners but did not explain the mechanism.

This matters because if the winners are partly luck, the strategy's future Sharpe is lower than 1.03. If they're skill, the strategy should generalize.

All walk-forward validation ends before 2025. We have no OOS data for the current regime. The ATR_RANK gate is failing silently. We won't know if the strategy is broken until we run live.
