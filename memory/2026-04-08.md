Built `examples/cross_sectional_laggard_short_wf.rs` — first strict OOS walk-forward test for the short-side sleeve (Track C, highest priority gap after 4+ sessions of docs).

Design: Long top-2 by 20d mom, Short bottom-1 simultaneously, BTC SMA200 regime gate, 9 universes × 252-bar train/252-bar test windows, 0.1% taker per side.

Results (56 windows across 8 universes):
- **Long-only: 13/56 = 23% OOS pass** (weak due to SMA200 suppressing signals in bear windows)
- **Short-only: 17/56 = 30% OOS pass** (slightly better — confirms short-side value)
- **MarketNeutral: 16/56 = 29% OOS pass**

Key finding: Short-only outperforms long-only (30% > 23%), confirming the short side adds value. But the SMA200 regime filter is too restrictive — it suppresses signals exactly when the short side is most valuable (bear regimes where BTC < SMA200). This is a design flaw: the strategy goes flat when it should be going short.

Next: Remove the SMA200 regime filter and re-test. A properly deployed cross-sectional short (without regime gate) may show stronger crisis-alpha. Also: test reducing short notional to 50% of long (less short drag in bull markets).
