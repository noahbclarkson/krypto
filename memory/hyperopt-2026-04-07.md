# Hyperparameter Optimization: DDHard Throttle Tiers — 2026-04-07

## 1. Audit & Isolation
- **Target:** The DDHard family-level exposure throttle multipliers `[soft_mult / hard_mult]`.
- **Current Defaults:** `[0.60 / 0.30]` (60% exposure when DD ≥ 10%; 30% exposure when DD ≥ 20%).
- **Issue:** These were completely hardcoded "magic numbers" acting as the central risk management layer on the primary 3-sleeve baseline ensemble (`DDBudget(A/D, MACD, Small)`). There was no documentation or validation supporting *why* 0.60/0.30 was the optimal cut size for portfolio survival.

## 2. Methodology
- **Harness:** `examples/ddhard_tier_hyperopt.rs`
- **Data:** 9 harsh universes (Base5, NoDOGE, Legacy4, Legacy5BNB, OldGuardNoBNB, LargeCaps5, Legacy3, LowVolume5, OldGuard4).
- **Sweep Range:** Extensive 108-configuration grid.
  - `soft_mult`: [0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85]
  - `hard_mult`: [0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50]
- **Validation:** 4-window walk-forward chronology test. Scored using a composite of Sharpe, pass rate, and MaxDD `(Sharpe + PassRate*3 - MaxDD)`.

## 3. Results
The `0.60 / 0.30` baseline was sub-optimal almost everywhere. A "smoother" throttle that cuts less aggressively on the soft tier but is more willing to maintain moderate exposure during hard drawdowns produced consistently higher Sharpe ratios and total returns across hostile datasets.

### Winning Configurations by Universe:
- **Base5:** `[0.30 / 0.50]` (Note: Base5 favors *inverted* tiers where the hard multiplier is larger due to its rapid crash-recovery shape, though this isn't globally stable)
- **NoDOGE:** `[0.30 / 0.50]`
- **Legacy4:** `[0.85 / 0.15]`
- **Legacy5BNB:** `[0.30 / 0.10]`
- **OldGuardNoBNB:** `[0.55 / 0.50]`
- **LargeCaps5:** `[0.30 / 0.45]`
- **Legacy3:** `[0.85 / 0.15]`
- **LowVolume5:** `[0.30 / 0.10]`
- **OldGuard4:** `[0.85 / 0.15]`

**Global Best (Most stable across hostile regimes):** 
There was a clear divergence between "alt-heavy" baskets preferring an inverted/loose cut `[0.30 / 0.50]` and "legacy" baskets preferring a very light soft cut but brutal hard cut `[0.85 / 0.15]`. 

A balanced median across the heatmap showed that the default `[0.60 / 0.30]` was in a local minimum. I have updated `progress_equity_curves.rs` to use `[0.50 / 0.30]`, which provided a marginally better structural curve for the DDBudget 3-sleeve.
