## Step 3: Key Structural Findings

### CHAND_MULT plateau (M∈[2.25..5.00])
M=2.25 through M=5.00 all produce **identical** results (avg Sharpe 6.844, pass rate 100%, trades 10.0). This is a **structural plateau** — once the multiplier is loose enough that Chandelier rarely fires first (Turtle ATR takes precedence), further increases are neutral. The practical implication: **M=2.25 is the tightest value at the plateau edge**, giving maximum stop protection before entering the neutral zone.

### CHAND_PERIOD phase transition (P≈30)
Below P≈30, Chandelier controls exit timing. Above P≈30, Turtle ATR becomes primary exit and CP no longer affects results. CP=11-19 is the optimal tight-stop cluster; CP=20-30 is the neutral plateau.

### Trade count compression
M=2.00: 10.2 trades/window avg; M=2.25: 10.0 trades/window avg. The tighter multiplier at P=11 does NOT meaningfully reduce trade frequency — it's the stop placement that matters, not the event count.

### No remaining hyperparameter to optimise
All production parameters are at or near their optimal values. The next phase of improvement requires either:
1. New signal/strategy (CTREND + Chandelier exit is the only remaining candidate with a Monte Carlo-validated signal)
2. Live execution data (maker-fill calibration, SOL slippage model)
3. Multi-timeframe entry (4h Turtle + daily Chandelier — untested combination, different from failed 4h MR)
