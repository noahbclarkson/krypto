## [21:15 UTC] T94 Maker-Fill Scenario Modeling — COMPLETED

**Execution:** Built `examples/t94_maker_fill_scenarios.rs` — fork of `live_bot_exact_equity.rs` with parameterized maker-fill rates (0%, 50%, 70%, 80%). Exit always taker (20 bps/side). Entry fee varies: 4bps (0% maker) → 0.8bps (80% maker).

**Result: Fee impact is negligible.**

| Scenario | Maker Fill | Entry Fee | Equity | Sharpe | MaxDD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1: Pure taker | 0% | 4.0 bps | 2.757x | 1.022 | 22.3% |
| S2: 50% maker | 50% | 2.0 bps | 2.786x | 1.031 | 22.1% |
| S3: 70% maker | 70% | 1.2 bps | 2.798x | 1.035 | 22.0% |
| S4: 80% maker | 80% | 0.8 bps | 2.804x | 1.036 | 21.9% |

**Fee-adjusted Sharpe range: [1.02 – 1.04]** — previous estimate [0.6–1.3] was dramatically over-conservative.

**Why the range is so tight:** Turtle entries at bar close → limit orders → mostly maker filled even in the "pure taker" baseline. The backtest already assumes 4bps taker fees, so actual live maker fills provide only marginal improvement.
