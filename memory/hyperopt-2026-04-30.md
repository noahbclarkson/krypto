# Hyperopt — ATR_ENTRY_MULT 201-Value Sweep (2026-04-30)

## Parameter Audited: ATR_ENTRY_MULT

**Why this parameter:** Every other Turtle+Chandelier hyperparameter has been swept and validated. `ATR_ENTRY_MULT` (the Turtle breakout entry filter — require breakout > `ATR_ENTRY_MULT × ATR` above recent high) had been tested on stale params (CHAND_P=11, EP=24) but never on current production params (CHAND_P=7, CHAND_MULT=2.30, EP=21). The previous sweep (2026-04-13) used EP=21 with older Chandelier values. We re-ran this to close the loop with correct current parameters.

## Scope

- **Range:** 0.00..=2.00 step 0.01 — **201 values** (extensive integer+frac sweep)
- **Universes:** 9 (Base5, NoDOGE, Legacy4, Legacy5BNB, OldGuardNoBNB, LargeCaps5, Legacy3, LowVolume5, OldGuard4)
- **Walk-forward windows:** 6 per universe (252-bar rolling, non-overlapping)
- **Total runs:** 201 × 9 × 6 = **10,854 walk-forward simulations**
- **Strategy:** Turtle+Chandelier(7, 2.30) / Turtle ATR(24, 2.0) dual exit
- **Fee model:** Correct (entry × (1+fee), exit × (1−fee)), TAKER_FEE=0.001

## Results

| Label | EM | Pass% | Avg Sharpe | Avg Ret% | Avg DD% | Trades |
|-------|-----|-------|-----------|----------|---------|--------|
| **Baseline (PROD DEFAULT)** | 0.00 | 74.1% | 3.147 | +105.3% | 35.4% | 721 |
| Robustness winner | 0.94 | **77.8%** (+3.7pp) | **5.345** (+69.8%) | +73.0% | 28.2% | 486 |
| Sharpe runner-up | 1.28 | 68.5% | 15.421 | +67.1% | 22.9% | 366 |

**Winner candidate:** EM=0.94 — highest pass rate (77.8%) AND highest pass-adjusted Sharpe (+69.8% vs baseline). Lower drawdown (28.2% vs 35.4%), fewer trades (486 vs 721) — more selective entry.

## Key Finding: Held-Out Rejection

EM=0.94 was identified as robustness winner in-session. However, **anti-overfit discipline applies**: the value was selected on the same walk-forward grid used for validation. Held-out validation (separate time period, pre-announcement) is required before promoting to production.

**Prior held-out result (2026-04-26):** ATR_ENTRY_MULT sweep with EP=21 / stale Chandelier: EM=0.85 won in-sample but FAILED held-out (10/18 vs baseline 11/18). Same inflation pattern.

**Conclusion:** Until a fresh held-out validation confirms EM=0.94, production default remains **ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.00** (no entry filter).

## Decision

**No production default change.** EM=0.00 remains. EM=0.94 is a genuine candidate but requires held-out validation on a pre-2021 or recent held-out time split before production promotion.
