### The Two Sharpe Problem
- Walk-forward avg Sharpe: 5.46-6.29 (average of per-window Sharpe ratios)
- Daily equity Sharpe: ~1.04-1.68 (actual compounded equity curve)
These measure fundamentally different things and are never comparable. We know this. But the reports/daily_progress.csv shows both together without explanation. Any reader would assume they're the same metric.

### Fee-Adjusted Sharpe: ~3.1-3.8 (Honest Range)
22-33% fee drag applied to gross walk-forward 5.46 gives 3.8. This is the most honest number for live expectations.

**Verdict:** Metrics are NOT fabricated — but the equity multiplier is unstable due to data refresh issues, and the dual-Sharpe problem creates misinterpretation risk.
