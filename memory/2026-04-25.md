### Issue 2: Metric Fragmentation Is Deceptive

We report 4 different Sharpe numbers and treat them interchangeably:
- Walk-forward OOS avg Sharpe: 5.46 (per-window, averaged — inflated)
- Daily equity compounded Sharpe: 1.68 (honest)
- DDBudget milestone-aggregated: 7.24 (not comparable to daily equity)
- ATR_entry_mult sweep: 6.12 (same inflated methodology as OOS avg)

The only honest number is the daily equity Sharpe (~1.0-1.3 range). The 5.46 is an artifact of the methodology — per-window Sharpe ratios averaged together don't measure the same thing as a real portfolio's Sharpe. Noah sees "Sharpe 5.46" and thinks we have a world-class strategy. We don't. We have a modest trend-following edge.

### Issue 4: We Only Have 310 Trades in 8 Years

310 trades on daily data is a very small sample for parameter inference. Every "optimal" param we find (EP=24, ATR=24, CP=7, CM=2.25) is optimized on ~310 samples per symbol. The 54-window walk-forward gives us more confidence, but even then — the parameter space is large and the number of windows is small. We may be overfitting without knowing it.

### Issue 5: We Test on Bull Markets and Call It Robustness

Look at the per-year returns: 2018 bear +1393%, 2022 bear +10.9%, 2021 chop +35.5%, 2023 +102%, 2024 +146%, 2025 +74%. The strategy wins BIG in bear crises (2018: +1393% vs BTC -65%). In bull years it's good but BTC often beats it (2023: Turtle +102% vs BTC +147%). This means: **the edge is crisis protection, not alpha generation**. We frame it as "robust trend-following" when it's actually "crisis alpha." That's a different value proposition.

### Issue 6: Our Biggest Blind Spot — Live Execution Is the Only Truth

Everything we have is simulated. The real world will tell us:
1. Maker-fill rate: assumed ~70%, actual unknown
2. Slippage: SOL assumed 1bp, actual likely 3-5bp at $50K notional
3. Signal latency: entries fire at bar close, fills arrive next bar — directional drift unknown
4. Exchange issues: rate limits, downtime, fill rejections

The walk-forward 83% pass rate means 17% of market regimes the strategy loses money. We don't know which regime we're in live. The pre-2021 stress (19/28 = 67.9%) is a more honest assessment — we fail ~30% of the time in adverse conditions.

## Top 3 Unbuilt Ideas (Genuinely Untried)

### 1. CTREND-Native Exit (Still Viable)

Monte Carlo confirmed CTREND signal is genuine (0/500 shuffled beat real). Prior test (#23) paired CTREND entry with Chandelier exit — wrong mechanism fit. The signal is slower (multi-horizon smoothing) and needs a longer-horizon exit.

**What to test:**
- Fixed hold sweep (10, 15, 21, 30, 45, 60 bars) × CTREND entry
- RSI exit (RSI>80 long, RSI<20 short) — different mechanism
- ATR longer exit (lookback 40-60, mult 2.5-3.0) — gives CTREND room to develop

**Baseline to beat:** Turtle+Chandelier 45/54 (83.3%). Any CTREND variant >40/54 is a viable signal family.

**Why this matters:** It's the only untested idea that produces a genuinely different signal family, not parameter tuning of Turtle.

### 2. Walk-Forward Correlation Entry Filter

**Concept:** Only enter Turtle on ALT symbols when BTC is also in a trend direction (e.g., `btc_turtle_signal == 1` or `eth_turtle_signal == 1`). Reduces whipsaw in BTC-silent regimes.

**What to test:**
- Run Turtle+Chandelier with and without BTC/ETH trend filter
- Sweep: no filter, BTC-only, BTC+ETH, any 1-of-2, all 2-of-2

**Hypothesis:** The 2026 YTD failure (-32.8% in the partial harness) might be BTC-led divergence. ALT breakouts without BTC confirmation get stopped out. BTC confirmation filter might reduce whipsaw.

**Risk:** Trade count reduction. If filter removes 30%+ of valid entries → net negative.

### 3. Live Execution Gap Monitor (No Backtest Possible)

Track live fill quality vs backtest assumptions in real-time. SOL is the known risk:
- Model: 1bp slippage at $50K notional
- Reality: likely 3-5bp (3-5x model)

**What to build:**
- Per-symbol slippage summary from FillLog CSV
- Running tally: actual vs model slippage per symbol
- Alert threshold: if SOL actual > 2x model → log alert, reduce SOL cap

**Why this matters:** It's the one gap between lab and live we cannot close with backtesting. The DDBudget "milestone-aggregated Sharpe 7.24" is meaningless in live context — we need per-fill execution quality tracking.
