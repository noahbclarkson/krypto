### Root Cause Analysis
The `df.column("atr")` EWM-based ATR from FeatureEngine is ~0.15-0.2% of price for 4h BTC. This is the **same ATR used in live production** which has been validated on daily data. The problem is NOT the ATR column — the daily strategy works because:

1. Daily bars have sufficient time for trends to develop between signal and exit
2. The daily Chandelier(P=7) tight trailing stop only works well when you have **21 days of daily close data** to trail
3. On 4h, 60 bars = 10 days = the entire lifetime of a typical trade, making the Chandelier essentially a fixed-time stop

The 4h Chandelier stop = the HOLD_MAX stop. They're equivalent timescales. This collapses the dual-exit mechanism to a single exit.

### Implication
The strategy is fundamentally tied to daily timeframe mechanics. Multi-timeframe Turtle would require **re-parameterizing the exit mechanism entirely** for 4h — not just scaling EP/CP by 6×. This is a deeper structural issue than a parameter tweak.

### Decision
**4h multi-timeframe is GRAVEYARD.** The idea was worth testing. The result is definitive: daily Turtle mechanics don't transfer to 4h without a complete structural rethink.
