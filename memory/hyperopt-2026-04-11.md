## Executive Summary

**Parameter:** Chandelier ATR Period (P) with Multiplier fixed at M=2.00  
**Winner:** P=28 (was P=15 from coarse step-5 sweep at M=2.5)  
**Improvement:** +15.7% Sharpe (9.011 vs 7.785)  
**Robustness:** P=28 beats P=15 in 9/9 universes (100%)  
**Validation:** DDBudget 3-sleeve passes 72% (unchanged, no regression)

---

## The Problem: Sequential Optimization Bias

1. **Coarse sweep (step 5)** at M=2.5 → found P=15 as winner
2. **Multipliers sweep** → found M=2.00 as winner (vs 2.5)
3. **P was NEVER re-swept** at the new M=2.00

The coarse sweep tested P=10,15,20,... but NOT P=16,17,18,19 or P=26,27,28. The true optimum was in the gaps between tested values.

## Key Lesson

**Never trust coarse-grid optimization results.** The optimal parameter often lies between the tested values. Fine-grained sweeps (step=1) are essential for finding true optima, especially when hyperparameters are correlated (P and M interact).
