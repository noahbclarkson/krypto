## [08:41 UTC] Perp-Perp Funding Carry — First Test

Built `examples/perp_perp_funding_carry.rs` from scratch — genuinely new lane, perp-perp carry without spot leg.

**Method:**
- Load 8h funding rates for BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, DOGE, XRP from `examples/funding_cache/*.parquet`
- Build daily funding snapshot (last known 8h rate per day)
- Compute rolling z-score per symbol over z_window days
- Long cheapest-funded (most negative z = underpriced shorts), short most expensive-funded (most positive z = overpriced longs)
- Hold fixed bars, 0.1% taker each side

**Results across 7 configs:**

| Config | Trades | Total% | Avg%/trade | DD | WR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| z_win=20, ez=1.5, hold=21 | 408 | +15268% | +1.242% | -92% | 54.2% |
| z_win=30, ez=1.0, hold=21 | 769 | +16635% | +0.668% | -100% | 54.0% |
| z_win=10, ez=1.0, hold=21 | 967 | +15852% | +0.526% | -102% | 50.9% |
| z_win=20, ez=0.5, hold=21 | 1438 | +1863% | +0.207% | -128% | 50.9% |
| z_win=10, ez=0.5, hold=10 | 1444 | -99.8% | -0.426% | -100% | 48.9% |
| z_win=20, ez=1.0, hold=10 | 842 | -99.5% | -0.629% | -102% | 50.4% |

**Critical observation: the SHORT leg consistently loses.** Every single config shows negative short leg (-4.3% to -6.7% avg per trade). This means expensive-funded assets tend to KEEP GOING UP — the carry hypothesis (funding rates mean-revert) is directionally wrong.

**Honest assessment:**
- This is TREND-FOLLOWING in disguise, not carry. The "short expensive" leg is just shorting recent winners.
- The long leg works because it's coincidentally long weak shorts (momentum on the short side too).
- DD of -92% to -128% is catastrophic — no promotion possible.
- BTC buy-hold over same period: +489% (vs 15,268% gross for the strategy — the numbers look big but they include massive leverage from compounding small per-trade edges with NO position sizing control).
- The strategy has no position sizing or risk management — full Kelly-equivalent compounding is meaningless.

**Verdict:** GRAVEYARD candidate. The carry hypothesis (funding rates as a mean-reversion signal) is falsified. The results are consistent with pure momentum on the long side.

**What next:** Fix and run the EWMA-CUSUM overlay harness (evidence stream is validated, just need th=10-15 range). Or try the leverage-fragility index with real funding data.
