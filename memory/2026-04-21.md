## Section 5: Biggest Blind Spots

### Blind Spot 1: ZERO 2026 OOS Data (CRITICAL)
All 54 walk-forward windows end before 2026. Our only actual 2026 regime data is -22.7% YTD with Sharpe -18.91. We don't know if the 83% pass rate holds in the current market. 2026 is our most important genuine OOS test and we haven't run it.

### Blind Spot 2: Bull Market Survivorship Bias (SEVERE)
The strategy's best years are mega-bull years (2018 +1393%, 2020 +879%). In normal bull (2023: +101.9% vs BTC +146.5%), Turtle LAGED BTC. In 2021 chop (+35.5%), barely won vs BTC -16.5%.
The per-year table shows Turtle beat BTC in 5/9 years. In 3 of those 5 winning years, BTC was in a severe bear. So the "edge" may largely be: Chandelier stops protect capital in drawdowns while buy-hold loses badly — not genuine alpha in all regimes.

### Blind Spot 3: Parameter Cycling / Hyperopt Redundancy (MODERATE)
ATR_MULT: swept 3× → same result each time.
ATR_PERIOD: swept 3× → same result each time.
CHAND_MULT: swept 3×.
EP: swept 4× → EP=21, EP=24, EP=21 again.
This pattern suggests we're not learning — confirmation sweeps look like progress but produce zero new knowledge.

### Blind Spot 4: Fee Model vs Live Slippage Reality (MODERATE)
SOL slippage at $100K = 3.70bp vs 1bp model = 3.7x miss. We cap SOL at $50K but the walk-forward never tested with explicit SOL sizing constraints. If actual SOL slippage at $50K hits 1.5-2x model, fee-adjusted Sharpe could be 0.5-1.0 lower than claimed.

### Blind Spot 5: Only 310 Trades Over 8 Years (LOW but accumulating)
310 trades in 8 years (~39/year). Each trade is binary. With 310 trades, 83% pass rate is based on sample where 1-2 bad years skew significantly. Equity curve dominated by mega-bull years.
