## Part 4: Our Biggest Blind Spots

### BLIND SPOT #1: ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.90 Dominates All Other Parameters

**Problem:** ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.90 is so effective that it makes Chandelier parameter validation impossible. With the filter ON (production), P=11, P=15, and P=20 all produce 63/63 pass. The filter is absorbing all the variance, and we can't tell if CHAND_PERIOD=11 is actually better than CHAND_PERIOD=15 under real conditions.

**Risk:** CHAND_PERIOD=11 might be inferior to CHAND_PERIOD=15 in a regime where ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.90 doesn't fire (e.g., low-vol chop where close rarely exceeds max_close + ATR*0.90). We have no way to test this because the filter always activates in trending regimes.

**Implication:** The Chandelier param sweep (P=11 vs P=15, M=2.25 vs M=1.50) was validated WITHOUT the ATR entry filter. Adding ATR_ENTRY_MULT after the fact may have introduced interaction effects we didn't measure. All CHAND param claims pre-2026-04-21 are valid WITHOUT the filter.

**Decision:** Keep ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.90 in production. Accept that Chandelier param claims (CP=11, M=2.25) are only validated for the no-filter engine. If live trading shows worse performance than backtest, the first diagnostic is to test with ATR_ENTRY_MULT=0.0 and compare.

### BLIND SPOT #2: Only 1 Window in 2026 — Sharpe Is Regime-Dependent

**Problem:** W06 (2026-01-01 to ~2026-04-08) is a single window with 14-20 trades per universe. The Sharpe values (7.79, 6.42, 9.79) are high partly because 2026 Q1 was a strong bull run. We have NO idea how the strategy performs in a sustained bear or chop regime in 2026.

**Historical pattern:** Per-year decomposition shows Sharpe inversely correlated with bull market strength. 2018 bear: Sharpe 2.07 (highest of any year). 2021 chop: Sharpe 0.76. 2022 bear: Sharpe 0.51. The strategy performs BEST in bear crises. The 2026 W06 result is from a bull phase — it may actually understate the strategy's bear performance.

**Decision:** Don't over-index on W06 bull result. The pre-2021 stress test (21/21 pass) covers bear regimes better than W06 does.

### BLIND SPOT #3: Equity Chart Verification — Still Not Done (T1)

**Problem:** T1 (equity chart column verification) has been in PLAN.md for 4+ sessions and is still NOT DONE. The most-viewed artifact in the project (the equity chart on Discord) has been wrong or unverified multiple times in project history.
