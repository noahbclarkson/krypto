Turtle+Chandelier in production generates ~80 trades across 6 symbols over ~7 windows. That's ~13 trades/symbol in the full OOS period. In a choppy market like W05 (FTX collapse), that's 14 trades in the Base5 window. Some of those are false breakouts. **We have no chop-filter.** The Chandelier stop handles it, but the equity drag from whipsawing in ranging markets is unmeasured.

The microstructure analysis showed ~70% maker fill in backtesting. But this was on HISTORICAL data. The live testnet has never been run. We don't know: (a) actual fill rates in live conditions, (b) slippage in fast markets, (c) whether the limit-order-at-close strategy actually works in real time. **All fee models are theoretical.**

**Blind Spot #4 — The equity harness is not the production system:**
`progress_equity_curves.csv` tracks 6 strategy families. Turtle+Chandelier's equity line was added as a separate CSV (`turtle_chandelier_equity.csv`) because the main harness used fixed 21-bar hold. This means Turtle+Chandelier is not in the unified equity monitoring. #### 4. BIGGEST BLIND SPOTS

Base5 = BTC ETH SOL XRP DOGE ADA. These are the top-6 crypto assets by market cap that SURVIVED every cycle. We excluded LTC/EOS/BCH because they fail W04/W05. **Blind Spot #2 — We only have ~8 years of data:**
Financial markets have 100+ years of equity data. We have 8 years of BTC, 5 years of ETH, 3 years of SOL. The strategy has NEVER been tested across a full credit cycle (high rates → recession → recovery). A 2026-2028 recession with 50%+ equity drawdown and rising rates would be a genuine unknown.

**Blind Spot #3 — Maker-taker gap is the last honest unknown:**
Every backtest assumes 70% maker fill. We have NEVER seen this strategy execute live. In a fast market (COVID crash, FTX collapse), maker fill rates drop dramatically. We might be 50% makers in normal conditions and 20% in crashes. The fee drag could be 2× what we modeled. **This is the only number that matters for live deployment.**

**Blind Spot #4 — Position cap of 3 is unvalidated outside Base5:**
CAP=3 was hyperopt-swept and won across 9 universes. But in Base5 production, the strategy never takes more than 3 positions simultaneously. With a starting capital of $10,000 and 3 positions at $3,333 each, a -35% drawdown on one position is -$1,167. This seems manageable. But in a 2018-style crash where ALL 3 positions are open and all 3 trend down, the Chandelier exit protects... but we haven't measured this scenario with SOL/ADA in the live data.

**Blind Spot #5 — No short-side validation:**
Every strategy in the repo is long-only. We tested shorts and they failed (MACD+Regime short side, cross-sectional momentum short side). The conclusion was "short side is a desert in crypto." This might be correct — but it means we have no strategy for bear regimes except reducing position size. The USDT hedge overlay (30% USDT when vol>75th pct) is the only risk mitigation tool. **What happens if the next crisis is a 12-month -75% drawdown with no meaningful bounces?** We don't know.
