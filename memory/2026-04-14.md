But in a 2018-style crash where ALL 3 positions are open and all 3 trend down, the Chandelier exit protects... but we haven't measured this scenario with SOL/ADA in the live data.

Every strategy in the repo is long-only. We tested shorts and they failed (MACD+Regime short side, cross-sectional momentum short side). The conclusion was "short side is a desert in crypto."
