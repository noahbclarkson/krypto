### IDEA 2 — BTC Trend Regime Filter for Altcoin Allocation (NEW)

**Concept:** Use BTC's own trend state (BTC > SMA200 bull / BTC < SMA200 bear) as a position-sizing input for the Turtle+Chandelier portfolio, not as an entry gate.

**Key distinction from failed approaches:** NOT "switch between A/D and Turtle based on BTC regime" (vol-rank switching failed). Instead: continuously scale Turtle position size based on BTC trend regime strength.

**Design:**

**Why it might work:** The USDT hedge overlay (30% USDT when BTC 21d vol > 75th pct) already showed DD improvement without pass-rate destruction. A trend-based scalar is a different mechanism — it's about reducing exposure in bear regimes, not just high-vol regimes.

### IDEA 3 — Trailing Stop Band System (ATR-Adaptive Exit)

**Concept:** Replace single Chandelier(28,2.0) with a **band** of trailing stops: Chandelier(28,2.0) + Keltner(20,2.0) + ATR_trailing_stop(N,1.5). Entry fires when price breaks above all three; exit fires when price hits ANY stop.

**Why this is different from vol-contingent Chandelier:** Instead of switching between Chandelier settings based on vol regime, we stack multiple stops at different sensitivities. The tightest stop (ATR×1.5) gets hit first in fast moves (protection), the loosest (Chandelier×2.0) lets trends run. The Keltner middle band provides a mid-level exit.

**Why promising:** Different stops catch different market dynamics. Turtle ATR(25) already contributes to the dual-exit (50/54 vs Chandelier-only 49/54). Adding a third stop could improve further.

**What to test:** Full 3×3 parameter sweep of band configurations × 9 universes × 54 windows.

**Risk:** More stops = more complexity = more overfitting surface. The contribution of Turtle ATR to dual-exit was only +2% Sharpe (marginal). A third stop may also be marginal.
