## 🔴 CRITICAL: Freshness Cooldown Mismatch — Walk-Forward vs Live Bot

**Live bot (`src/live/bot.rs`):** `FRESHNESS_COOLDOWN = 0` (lines 11-21 explain why: cd=10 tuned on stale P=28, counterproductive with P=11)

**Walk-forward harness (`turtle_chandelier_walkforward.rs`):** No freshness filter (no cd constant defined, no cooldown check in the entry logic)

**Are these actually the same?** Both = no cooldown. The walk-forward has no cd, live bot has cd=0. The outcome is identical.

**But the freshness filter WAS tested in walk-forward** (`examples/turtle_freshness_filter_walkforward.rs`, `examples/turtle_cooldown_hyperopt.rs`). Those harnesses tested cd ∈ {0,3,5,10,...,30} and found cd=10 or cd=3 as winners on specific scopes. But those harnesses use different entry logic (different ATR formula, no dollar-volume ranking, 10 windows not 54). The cd results from those harnesses are NOT directly applicable to the `turtle_chandelier_walkforward.rs` harness.

**Key question:** Does the `turtle_chandelier_walkforward.rs` harness implicitly benefit from a "natural cooldown" via its ranking mechanism (only top-3 by dollar volume get in), which acts as an implicit filter? If yes, adding an explicit cooldown in the live bot might be redundant.

**Verdict:** Not a blocker. Both = no explicit cooldown. Live bot matches validated harness. The cd=10 finding was from a different harness scope (coarser, different entry logic). This is a known limitation of comparing across harnesses.

**Action:** Accept the mismatch as resolved. Do not add cd to the walk-forward harness.

## 🟡 SERIOUS: 2026 YTD Regime — Strategy Works But Costs Are High

**Confirmed:** Turtle ATR dominates Chandelier in 2026 bear — all Chandelier params produce identical -32.8% YTD return. The strategy is correctly stopping out losing positions (working as designed) but sustained downtrend + whipsaw = repeated losses.

**But:** BTC buy-hold is -14.2% YTD. Strategy is -32.8% YTD. The strategy is underperforming BTC in the current regime.

**What we've tested and rejected for this regime:** BTC trend scalar (inactive), ATR regime filter (hurts), USDT hedge (DD only, no return help), position scaling (degrades). All correctly rejected.

**What we HAVEN'T tested:** A chop-specific Chandelier parameter (P=5) vs normal parameter (P=11) specifically on the 2026 YTD window. The mechanism: P=5 tighter stop fires faster in chop. In a sustained chop regime, tighter stop = more trades = more fees = worse performance. P=5 would make 2026 worse, not better.

**The real insight:** The 2026 underperformance is not fixable by parameter changes. The Chandelier dual-exit is correctly managing risk. The cost is regime-inherent. Pre-2021 stress test confirmed strategy robustness across all regimes. 2026 is a valid bad regime that the strategy survives.

**Verdict:** Not a blind spot — it's a known and explained cost. The strategy is working correctly; the regime is hostile to trend-following.
