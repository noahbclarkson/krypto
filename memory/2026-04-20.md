### Key Mechanism Insight

CTREND is a smoothed multi-horizon momentum signal (5/21/63/126-bar returns normalized by vol). Turtle is a breakout signal (close > max close of last 21 bars). 

In trending markets, Turtle captures the initial breakout momentum — the price is already moving, and the Chandelier trailing stop locks in profits quickly. CTREND's smoothed signal fires later (after momentum builds across multiple horizons), by which point the best part of the trend may already be captured by Turtle entries from earlier bars.

The CTREND signal is genuinely predictive (Monte Carlo confirmed 0/500 shuffled beat real), but it doesn't synergize with Chandelier dual-exit as well as Turtle breakout does. This is a real finding: the signal quality is real, but the signal-exit combination matters enormously.

## What This Means

1. **CTREND is a genuine signal** — Monte Carlo (0/500 shuffled beat real) confirmed this. The ~1400x equity on the old progress chart represented real signal quality, not look-ahead artifact.

2. **CTREND is not a Turtle replacement** — The signal fires less frequently and with worse timing for Chandelier dual-exit. CTREND's multi-horizon smoothing is a different timing mechanism that doesn't complement the Chandelier tight trailing stop as well as Turtle's breakout does.

3. **The fixed 21-bar hold was the CTREND equity chart's real flaw** — Not the signal itself. But fixing the exit (Chandelier) doesn't make CTREND outperform Turtle. The entry signal matters too.

4. **No second signal family from CTREND** — Turtle+Chandelier remains the only validated production strategy.
