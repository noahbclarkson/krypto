**Key finding: T ∈ [62-66] is a robust plateau** — all produce 54-55/63 pass (85.7-87.3%), Sharpe 5.6-6.6. T=65 selected as midpoint of plateau.

**Why T=65 wins over T=5:**
- Pass rate: 55/63 (87.3%) vs 52/63 (82.5%) — +4.8pp
- Sharpe: 6.594 vs 4.509 — +46.3%
- Avg return: 132.7% vs 105.4% — +27.3pp
- Mechanism: T=65 only enters when BTC is in the top 35% of historical ATR — most trending/volatile regimes. T=5 enters at top 95% — almost always, providing almost no filtering. T=65's filtering eliminates the weakest choppy-regime setups, improving Sharpe substantially.

**Note on T > 90:** Sharpe explodes to infinity because very few windows have any trades at those thresholds. T > 90 produces 0-37 passes and is numerically unstable. T ∈ [0..90] is the stable sweep range.

## Analysis: T=65 vs T=5 — Why the Difference?

The ATR rank threshold gates entries based on BTC's current volatility regime:
- **T=5:** Only blocks entries when BTC ATR is in the bottom 5% of its 252-bar history — essentially never fires, so the filter is inert
- **T=65:** Only allows entries when BTC ATR is in the top 35% — filters out low-volatility chop regimes where Turtle breakout signals are whipsawed

At T=65, the strategy is effectively regime-conditional: it only takes positions during trending/high-vol regimes (which is exactly when Turtle breakout signals have the most edge). This explains the +46% Sharpe improvement — not more trades, but much higher quality trades.

**The mechanism is sound and intuitive.** T=65 is "only enter when BTC volatility is elevated relative to its recent history."
