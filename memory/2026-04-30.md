## [09:58 UTC] Hyperopt summary — TAKER_FEE effective-cost audit

Audited validated Turtle+Chandelier hardcoded constants and selected `TAKER_FEE` as the assumption to test. This uncovered a real harness issue: `turtle_chandelier_walkforward.rs` uses `entry = entry_px * (1 - fee)` and `exit = exit_px * (1 - fee)`, so the hardcoded fee cancels in `exit / entry - 1`. The claimed 0.1% taker fee was effectively not applied.

Built `examples/fee_sweep_walkforward.rs` with correct cost accounting (`entry*(1+fee)`, `exit*(1-fee)`) and swept 0..20 bps/side step 1 bp across 9 universes × 6 WF windows. Built `charts/plot_fee_sweep.py`; generated `charts/comparison_chart.png` as a real line chart of Base5 OOS equity curves.

Results: pass rate stayed 34/54 for all fee levels. Avg Sharpe degrades smoothly from 3.392 at 0 bps → 3.303 at live 4 bps → 3.170 at 10 bps → 2.950 at 20 bps. Avg return degrades +113.6% → +111.2% → +107.8% → +102.2%. No strategy default changed; fee is an execution assumption, not an alpha hyperparameter. Future harnesses should fix fee accounting before citing fee-adjusted metrics.
