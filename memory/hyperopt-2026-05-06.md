## Verdict

`FRESHNESS_COOLDOWN` is not a free improvement. Long cooldowns around 53-58 bars materially improve walk-forward pass rate and reduce drawdown by cutting repeated same-symbol churn, but they also reduce full-history Base5 convexity/return by filtering many re-entries.

I am **not promoting** `FRESHNESS_COOLDOWN=58` to production yet, despite the robustness win, because:

1. The exact production HOF harness (`live_bot_exact_equity.rs`) remains the source of truth and currently does not parameterize/export cooldown variants.
2. The cooldown winner sacrifices large trend-following convexity in the Base5 full-history curve.
3. Given known top-trade concentration, a 58-day re-entry block could accidentally exclude rare convex winners.

Stable default remains: `FRESHNESS_COOLDOWN = 0`.

Next required gate before any promotion: add cooldown parameterization to the exact source-of-truth replay and run a top-10 winner skip audit for CD=53/55/58 vs baseline.
