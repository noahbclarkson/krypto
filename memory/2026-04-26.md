### S4: Vol-Adaptive Chandelier (Structural Rethink) — UNTESTED
Not a parameter tweak. A structural change.
- Current: Chandelier(P=7, M=2.30) is static
- Idea: When realized volatility > 75th percentile of 252-bar history → widen multiplier (M=3.0+, looser stop). When vol < 25th percentile → tighten (M=1.75, tighter stop).
- Why this might work: In choppy high-vol regimes (like 2026 YTD), the current tight Chandelier fires constantly. A wider stop would hold positions through noise.
- Previous attempt (vol-contingent multiplier, 2026-04-12) FAILED — all configs produced identical results. But that test used a 21-bar realized vol rank which is too slow-moving. A 252-bar rank (matching our ATR baseline) might behave differently.

### S5: Turtle Entry Only, No Chandelier — UNTESTED
The dual-exit (Chandelier OR Turtle ATR fires first) is our validated mechanism.
But: in high-vol regimes, both exits fire too early. What if we test:
- Entry: Turtle breakout (EP=21)
- Exit: ONLY Turtle ATR(24, 2.0) — no Chandelier
- Hold until Turtle ATR exit
This is a single-exit strategy. It might survive choppy regimes better because Turtle ATR(24) is slower to fire than Chandelier(P=7, M=2.30).
The risk: Chandelier is what makes the strategy work in trending markets. Removing it might break the edge.
